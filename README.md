## Usage

Head to productivityfolder.com to use the web hosted version. To run locally run "npm run dev" in the root folder then open the browser to address it tells you.


<img width="541" height="214" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7c2352c5-5094-4e5f-ac21-7fd6bf4fe717" />

Once signed in, begin by adding Groups or Tasks to your folder in the top right.



<img width="1105" height="546" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ee5731af-8c83-4f78-823c-a65cca171cec" />

Click on the created tabs to choose which group of tasks to display.



<img width="820" height="1243" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6625c89b-8adb-41ff-867d-00a26842808b" />

On mobile or smaller screens, you will see a selector rather than tabs, use it to choose the group to display. 

Once a task is completed it, it will be shaded in green. The tasks will reset each day/week/month etc at midnight, depending on what frequency you chose.
Non repeating tasks will remain there in green until deleted. 


<img width="202" height="249" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/571c3343-41f6-4045-8dc7-9665e7f8297f" />

You can use these buttons on the side to Complete, Edit or Delete the relevant task. 


<img width="583" height="466" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f261d824-d8e5-4578-806a-3f77128b3a4e" />

For groups, you can open the menu by clicking on the three dots in the top right corner, here you can edit or delete the group. 
If you delete a group with tasks inside, you will be prompted to either save the corresponding tasks, or delete them with the group.
If you choose to save them, they will be moved to the "Ungrouped" tab until you choose to move or delete them.


<img width="390" height="415" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/02c6776b-bfb7-4da4-9a61-38b445d8f78b" />

Clicking on the name of a task will bring up a calendar showing you all the days over the last 4 weeks where you successfully completed the task.
